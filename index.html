Xiao Zhang

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science, UT Austin · UTNS

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by Daehyeok Kim. I build networked and distributed systems for AI and latency-critical applications. My research spans datacenter networks and edge computing, with a current focus on making AI workloads predictable and efficient—from the network fabric up to GPU resources. My goal is to build practical systems that bridge real-world deployment with the infrastructure needs of modern AI.
